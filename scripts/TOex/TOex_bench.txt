 2.2  Where EMOG(D) puts melt XCO₂, and why a cap exists
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
      The buffer DOES dictate melt XCO₂ from P-T-fO2 (invert_carbon_buffer_fO2); XCO₂ = aC·Bmelt.
      A ceiling is needed because at EMOG(D) the buffer already asks for essentially pure CO₂.

       T [K]   P [GPa]   EMOG(D)    Bmelt at EMOG   at EMOG-1   at EMOG-2
        1273       2.0    -10.242           0.769      0.0769     0.00769
        1500       3.0     -7.090           1.458      0.1458     0.01458
        1673       5.0     -4.857           1.414      0.1414     0.01414
        1900       6.0     -3.035           2.064      0.2064     0.02064

      Bmelt > 1 is not a composition; with nC_melt = XCO₂·nOx/(1-XCO₂) it is an unbounded sink.

      Sweep: median XCO₂ = 1.1e-9, p99 = 0.089, max = 0.292; 1.9 % of cases solve at/above EMOG.
      The cap is dormant in normal use.

 4. ROBUSTNESS TO A PATHOLOGICAL SOLID BRANCH
===============================================================================================
    Motivated by poor convergence below ~3 GPa and low T with REAL MAGEMin. The mock was made
    pathological four ways to find which reproduces it.
    48 cases: T {400,600,900,1500} K x P {0.1,0.5,1,3} GPa x TOex {1e-4,1e-3,1e-2}.

      solid fO2(Fe³⁺) behaviour            before fix     after fix
      monotonic (baseline)                    47/48         47/48
      NON-MONOTONIC (dip from phase change)   31/48         47/48
      sparse (most points dropped)            47/48         47/48
      nearly flat (little fO2 sensitivity)    47/48         47/48

    Only NON-MONOTONICITY breaks it, and badly: uniqueness needs every demand to rise with fO2, so
    a dip — what an assemblage change looks like — makes the inversion ill-posed and the root vanish.
    FIX: isotonic regression (pool-adjacent-violators), least-squares monotone, ordered points
    untouched; a negligible increasing ramp breaks the ties pooling creates. Warns when it engages.

 5. WHAT IS DATA, WHAT IS CHOSEN, WHAT IS EMERGENT

 5.1  External data used INSIDE the solve
      solid fO2 vs Fe³⁺        MAGEMin sb24 (Stixrude & Lithgow-Bertelloni 2024), computed live
      melt Fe³⁺ oxybarometer   Hirschmann (2022) regression
      melt CO₂ buffer          Stagno & Frost-style: 5.44 - 21380/T + 0.078(P-1)/T + log XCO₂
      fluid K(T)               Holland & Powell (1998) via Perple_X hp02ver.dat
      EMOG(D) boundary         hp02ver.dat magnesite + enstatite + forsterite
      fluid fugacities         Zhang & Duan (2009): 15 EOS coefficients, LJ eps/sigma, mixing k
      melt integral-VdP        Sun & Yao (2024) / Deng et al. (2020), via sf_H2O.jl
      endmember compositions   harzburgite / basalt mass fractions

 5.2  External data used for REPORTING ONLY
      logfO2_FMQ               sb24 FMQ (3fa + O2 = 3qtz + 2mag); matches MAGEMin's own dQFM
      logfO2_IW                sb24 IW (4Fe + 2O2 = Fe4O4 — SLB's wuestite end-member is Fe4O4)
      slb_gibbs                one sb24 end-member's G(P,T) from Table C1 — Mie-Grueneisen-Debye
                               on a BM3 cold curve, Newton on V, plus metal electronic (A2-A3)
                               and bcc-Fe magnetic (A5-A9) terms
      slb_gibbs_O2             O2 standard state, sb24's JANAF-fitted Cp polynomial
      slb_plg                  Debye free-energy integral (HeFESTo/Perple_X form)
      Rasinhspace              signed Fe3+/SumFe axis, uniform in asinh(R/R0) — crosses R = 0,
                               which the logit cannot; beats splitting the table at 0 by ~9x
      enforce_R_monotone!      isotonic repair at table-GENERATION time (sf_H2O.jl)
      reference_buffers        the (FMQ, IW) pair reported against; needs no MAGEMin
      logfO2_*_frost           Frost (1991) FMQ and IW, retired — kept for the scripts (see 1.8)
      logfO2_FMQ_hp98          hp02ver FMQ, retained for comparison (see 1.7)

 5.3  Numbers CHOSEN, not measured — in order of leverage
      XCO2max = 0.3     Melt CO₂ cap, stand-in for CO₂ saturation. INVENTED. Does NOT replace
                        EMOG(D) — it only bounds where the inversion stops being physical, since
                        nC_melt diverges as X -> 1 and EMOG wants XCO₂ ~ 0.8-2.1 (see 2.2).
                        Never binds across the sweep (worst raw/cap 0.994): insurance, not active.
      wEMOG = 0.25      Width of the graphite -> carbonate ramp. INVENTED but motivated: real
                        carbonate is a magnesite-dolomite-calcite solution, so the boundary is smeared.
      slope 1/0.1917    Oxybarometer extrapolation beyond both table ends. Semi-physical: the
                        dilute-limit Fe³⁺/Fe²⁺ exponent the melt branch already uses.
      Rmax, nr, R0      Fe³⁺ sampling range, node count, asinh scale (0.4, 100+15, 2e-3).
      sharpness = 30    Softcap rounding.
      1e-6 tie-break    Strictly-increasing ramp after isotonic repair.
      tol = 1e-8        Mass-balance residual: above bisection's ~1e-11 floor, far below the
                        ~0.1-3.5 jump at a genuine discontinuity. Eight orders of daylight.
      lowclip, brackets, iteration counts — numerical; tested not to bind on the answer.

 5.4  Emergent — solved for, never prescribed
      the shared fO2 and hence ΔFMQ; aC; sOₑₓ and mOₑₓ; melt XCO₂; the fluid's mole fractions and

      sink; which sinks pin; the regime label.

 6. OPEN ISSUES
===============================================================================================
  1. XCO2max = 0.3 invented, but measured inactive across the sweep (worst raw/cap 0.994). Matters
     only near and above EMOG(D). See 5.3, 2.2.
  2. Low-P/T failures with real MAGEMin are reproduced by non-monotonic solid fO2 in the mock and
     repaired (§4), but the real cause is unconfirmed. Run real MAGEMin, watch for the warning.
  3. No precipitating Fe³⁺ phase. Believed unnecessary (§3.3) but untested above the tabulated
     range, where the extrapolation cannot capture a phase change MAGEMin would have found.
  4. Zhang & Duan (2009) is calibrated 673-2573 K and <=10 GPa; outside that the fluid is
     extrapolated and the code warns.
  5. All sweep results use the mock solid branch. Real MAGEMin now runs, so this is a re-run.
  6. RESOLVED FOR REPORTING 2026-09-03 — depth-driven self-reduction. Disproportionation conserves
     oxygen so the Frost mechanism sits at sOₑₓ = 0 and cannot DRIVE the solver, but metal is now
     harvested from the assemblage and reported (FeM_solid, split FeM_diss / FeM_disp). §1.8.
  7. Metal onset ~12 GPa vs a quoted ~250 km (~8 GPa), and the 20 GPa assemblage drops metal
     between two metal-bearing neighbours. Both unexplained. §1.8.
  8. Fux et al. (2024) benchmarked (§1.8). What survives the buffer fix: sb24 +0.06 to +1.86 too
     oxidised at their bulk Fe³⁺ (mean 0.99, was 2.48) and holds too little ferric at saturation
     (A 0.07 → 0.63; C 0.13 → 0.56). CAUSE: garnet's only ferric end-member is andradite, so Fe³⁺
     rides on calcium (x3 CaO → x8.9 ceiling), and opx has none. NOT the alloy — 5 GPa would need
     a(Fe) = 0.006. Fix is upstream: skiagite in gtmj, a ferric opx end-member.
  9. RESOLVED 2026-09-03 — reference buffers. FMQ and IW now come from sb24, the dataset producing
     the fO2 they are subtracted from. Removed a +1.7-to-+4.0 pressure-growing ΔFMQ error and
     stopped ΔIW putting metal-bearing rock above its own buffer. TOex.jl §4c, buffer_check.jl.
     G is computed in TOex.jl from Table C1/C3, not through LibMAGEMin.G_EM_function, so the
     buffers need no live handle and cannot segfault. Printed values cost <=0.0084 on FMQ,
     <=0.0012 on IW. Learned in passing:
       - SLB's `wu` is Fe4O4, not FeO (`pe` likewise Mg4O4), so the reaction is per TWO O2.
       - MAGEMin's `iw`/`qif` are UNUSABLE with sb: they call an end-member `iron` sb24 lacks and
         G_EM_function SEGFAULTS on unknown names. Stoichiometry is wrong too.
       - MAGEMin reads sb24's O2 Cp `b` from a negated slot (+6.91e-4 where HP have -6.91e-4).
         TOex.jl matches it deliberately: cancels in any offset, keeps ΔFMQ/ΔIW exact, moves the
         absolute buffer by 0.02-0.06.
       Both MAGEMin issues worth reporting upstream.
 10. sb24's iron redox scale sits ~1.4 log units ABOVE experimental 1-bar IW (-11.50 vs O'Neill's
     -12.90 at 1400 K). MECHANISM: MAGEMin's order-disorder slot (`cme`) is ZERO for every
     end-member, so Table C3's Landau term is never applied and wuestite is short R·ln5 per Fe²⁺
     = 1.398 log units, independent of P and T. It CANCELS in ΔIW (fayalite is short the same),
     which is why metal-bearing rock still lands physical — do NOT add it to IW alone.
     It does not cancel where the ABSOLUTE fO2 meets the HP98/Zhang & Duan fluid: 1.4 of the ~1.9
     offset in 1.5 is now accounted for. Fixing it means restoring the Landau term for ALL ferrous
     end-members — an upstream MAGEMin change.
