 A.0  WHERE THE CODE LIVES   (refactor done 2026-08-31, before Phase 0)
 -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   volatiles_mod.f90      module volatilestuff   variables, tracer field indices, budgets, the
                                                 &volatiles namelist, and the TOex solver. A LEAF.
   volatileprocs_mod.f90  module volatileprocs   the ~16 volatile procedures, out of tracers.f90.

   ! The two MUST stay in separate files: comppars reads the volatile master switches and
     volatileprocs needs comppars, so one file is an object-level cycle. Verified by building it.

   STAYED BEHIND, DELIBERATELY
     eval_fO2_traWsol, compute_H2O_hzbs   need perplexstuff, whose closure includes io and
       meltprocs, and meltprocs calls volatileprocs - importing it back reintroduces the cycle.
     traceelement_water/carbon            comppars reads the whole traceelement_* family as a block.
     Dpartition_* / outgas_fraction_*     six members of one 60-entry family; splitting fragments
       the input file for no gain. B.5 still applies - see 2.2.

   prem_pressure / prem_depth moved meltprocs -> miscellaneous to break the solub_* arm.
   ! NEW PARAMETERS GO IN &volatiles, NOT &tracersin.

 A.1  THE THREE LEVELS   - each quantity lives at exactly one
 -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   TRACER   every conserved quantity, and H2O solubility.
            H2O, H2Ofree, OxC, ReC, OEX, CFLUID, OFLUID, WSOL, fO2, F3FT, SCRB
   CELL     the TOex solve and nothing else. Inputs summed from the cell's tracers AFTER
            solubility; outputs written back onto them.
            ? Why not per tracer: a tracer is either solid or molten, so it could only ever pass
              phi = 0 or 1 - the degenerate branches where one Fe3+ host is ABSENT, and the
              solid<->melt Fe3+ exchange would not exist. The cell is the smallest volume in which
              the coexistence being solved for is represented.
   GRID     derived intensive state, cached for the step, never advected.
            fO2, the five COH mole fractions, rho_fluid, eta_fluid.
            ! Diagnostics and coefficients only. Nothing conservative lives here.

 A.2  WHERE THE SOLVE SITS IN THE TIMESTEP
 -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   The slot exists inside TraMeltErupt; the two eval_fO2_traWsol calls will be replaced.
       1  flow solve
       2  carbon ingassing            (new velocities, before advection)
       3  advect tracers
       4  melting / eruption          (solidus reads C(c_oxC), lagged one step - B.9)
       5  per-tracer solubility       S_eff = S*a_H2O^n, a_H2O LAGGED from step n-1     3.C.1
       6  gather -> CELL TOex solve   -> fO2, sink shares, speciation, rho, eta        3.B.1 3.C
       7  scatter back to tracers     incremental for rock, proportional for fluid     3.B.2 3.D
       8  outgassing                  speciated, from 6                                3.F.1
       9  COH redistribution / Darcy  on the (H,C,O_ex) triple, rho and eta from 6     3.E
      10  tracer -> grid interpolation, for output and for step n+1's solidus

   ! No grid->tracer interpolation of speciation is needed: rho and eta are consumed as cell
     scalars in the same cell the solve ran in.
   ! Fluid arriving in a new cell is not re-speciated until the next solve. Harmless for
     conservation precisely because OFLUID is transported explicitly, not recomputed on arrival.
   ! TWO LAGS, both structural and both one step: a_H2O into 5 (B.16) and the carbonate fraction
     into 4's solidus (B.9). Neither is a shortcut - each output is produced by 6, downstream of
     its consumer. Removing either needs an inner iteration over the whole step.

 A.3  RUNTIME ALGORITHM, TABLED MAGEMIN
 -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   ? A full lookup of the TOex outputs was rejected: the input space is 7-D (1.3e9 nodes at 20 per
     dimension), and the regime boundaries are near-discontinuities, so interpolating across one
     returns shares that do not sum to 1 - and renormalising breaks the shared-fO2 construction.

   EXPENSIVE AND TABULATABLE   MAGEMin. Depends only on (P,T,p,R). Already in the fork as
                               fO2_data x 6 maps + metal_data x 6.
   NOT TABULATABLE, AND NOT CHEAP EITHER. Measured on the ported solver, 2000 calls at phi = 1
   over 2-22 GPa and 1200-2700 K, all converged:
         1.14 ms per cell        34 outer bisection iterations, from the full bracket
   Against the 2-D benchmark (1 x 256 x 64 = 16384 cells, 8.2 s/step as it stands today):
         every cell, every step, cold start   18.7 s/step   2.3x THE ENTIRE CURRENT TIMESTEP
   ! COST CONTROL IS NOT OPTIONAL AND IS NOT PREMATURE. Three levers, in order of what they buy:
     (1) CONVERGENCE TOLERANCE, the big one. The bisection currently runs until the RESIDUAL is
         tiny; 34 halvings of a ~25 log unit bracket is ~1e-9 in fO2, against a TABLE whose own
         resolution is ~0.01 (A.4). Exiting at the table's resolution is worth roughly 3x. This is
         1.6's deferred "revisit as a perf change after GATE 1".
     (2) WARM START. Seeding from the cell's stored fO2POS narrows the bracket from ~25 log units
         to ~4, which is log2(25/4) = 2.6 HALVINGS - about 3 iterations, not 25. Bisection is
         logarithmic in bracket width, so a warm start is worth much less here than intuition
         suggests. It is still free and cannot change the answer, but it is not the lever.
     (3) DIRTY-FLAG SKIPPING (3.C.5). Linear in the fraction skipped, so it is the only lever that
         can buy an order of magnitude - and after (1) and (2) the step is still ~6 s against a
         current 8.2. EXPECT TO NEED IT. It is sequenced last because it is the only one that
         changes the answer, not because it is unlikely.

   Worth building later, both low-dimensional: a (P,T) table of K1..K4, EMOG, the gph/dia sign,
   IDV and FMQ; and optionally phi_i(P,T) as an EOS seed to cut the 6 fixed passes to 2.

 A.4  THE SOLID TABLES: AXIS, RESOLUTION, FILE CONTRACT
 -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   THE AXIS IS THE WHOLE PROBLEM. The oxybarometer is log-linear in Fe3+/Fe2+ = R/(1-R), so
   sampling uniformly in R fights a logarithm. Max |error| in log10 fO2, R in [1e-3, 0.185]:
         nR      uniform-in-R    log-spaced
         17          1.67           0.032
         25          1.24           0.014
         49          0.68           0.0036
         65          0.51           0.0020
   Uniform cannot reach the target at any practical node count. No byte budget buys past a wrong axis.

   ? On a positive-only axis Rmin is 1e-3, not 0, because fO2 -> -inf as R -> 0. The signed axis
     supersedes it.
   ! THE R CEILING IS MAGEMin'S, NOT A CHOICE. sb24 stops returning finite fO2 somewhere above the
     sampled top; on the current composition that is beyond R = 0.4, which is what the table uses.
   ! CONSEQUENCE FOR THE DEEP MANTLE, unchanged: bridgmanite wants Fe3+/SumFe ~ 0.5-0.7, above
     anything tabulated, so lower-mantle fO2 rests on the log-linear EXTRAPOLATION. Same form
     TOex.jl falls back on, so the two stay consistent - but GATE 1 must test it, not assume it.

   (P,T)      100 x 100 per sector. Decimation tests put 100x100 near 0.008 RMS; finer buys little
              because the MAX error is set by phase-assemblage discontinuities and does not converge.
   precision  6 decimals for fO2 (1e-6 log units), 8 for metal (it runs down to ~1e-5).
   size       ~26 MB, ~2.94e6 minimizations, ~30 min on 16 threads. Generation time is the constraint.

   FILE CONTRACT. The R axis is written EXPLICITLY as nR values on header line 5. Reconstructing
   it from min/max as an even grid silently mislocates every interior node. A legacy nR=1 table
   whose line holds "Rmin Rmax" still loads (list-directed read takes the first value).

   THE TABLE PAIR AS OF 2026-09-03 - two files, generated together, cross-validated at load:
       StagfO2.dat    100 x 100 x 30, R in [-1.96, 0.40], uniform in asinh(R/2e-3) to 2.7e-15
       StagMetal.dat  identical grid and map order, native Fe as a WEIGHT FRACTION of the solid
     load_s_fO2_data aborts if the metal grid or R axis disagrees - they would describe different
     rocks. A MISSING metal table is not fatal; the solver degrades to FeM_net.
     ! THE TWO ALWAYS REGENERATE TOGETHER. They come off the same minimisations, so a mismatched
       pair is not a partial update, it is two different rocks.
     ! 120 of 1.8M nodes non-finite: the SAME 2 (P,T) cells in the TZ sector at T ~ 3970-4000 K,
       every R, both endmembers. Fully molten, benign, filled from finite neighbours at load.

   INTERPOLATION COORDINATE. u = log(R/(1-R)) on a positive-only axis, asinh(R/R0) on a signed one
   (the logit is undefined at R <= 0). Interpolating in R instead throws away the axis.
   ! Metal is CLAMPED in R at both ends where fO2 is not - there is no metal above the sampled
     range and no more of it below, so extrapolating either end invents iron.

   LOADER CONTRACT, three rules the multi-R path depends on:
     (a) thermochem_int's Hint means H2O for 'solubility' and R for 'fO2'. It is clamped to the
         solubility table's H2O axis ON THE SOLUBILITY PATH ONLY - clamping it on the fO2 path
         pins R to the H2O axis, which has one node.
     (b) Non-finite nodes are repaired at load from finite neighbours, with a warning, and abort
         if a node has no finite neighbour. Untouched they poison the four cells around each.
     (c) Maps read are COUNTED, so a truncated file is a diagnosed abort rather than whole maps
         left undefined.

   ! GATE 1 TRAP. TOex.jl builds its own interpolant on a much finer axis than the table, so at
     low R the FORTRAN IS MORE ACCURATE THAN THE REFERENCE and they will disagree by far more than
     0.01. Settle the comparison basis before GATE 1 runs, or it measures the wrong thing.

 PART B - DECISIONS TAKEN
===============================================================================================

 B.1  REFERENCE STATE
 -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   Excess oxygen against Fe as FeO, C as graphite, H as H2O:
       CO2 / carbonate +2 per C · CO +1 · organic C, graphite 0 · CH4 -2 · H2O 0 · H2 -1 per H2
   Mass conversion: 2 O per C = 2*15.999/12.011 = 2.6642 kg O per kg C.
   ! EVERY entry and exit point uses this convention, through ONE helper (B.6). If it is
     duplicated, the budget drifts and the failure looks like a solver problem.

 B.2  CARBON-OFF COMPATIBILITY IS A GATING GUARANTEE, NOT A PHYSICS IDENTITY
 -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   With traceelement_carbon = .false. the solver is never called, the tables are never loaded
   (refstate.f90 passes traceelement_carbon to load_s_fO2_data), and every new field sits inside
   `if (traceelement_carbon)`. So CFLUID == 0, OFLUID == 0, a_H2O == 1 and the redistribution
   behaves exactly as today.
   ! A CARBON-FREE FLUID IS NOT A PURE-H2O FLUID, and its O_ex is not zero. Measured with Mc = 0
     at 3 GPa / 1500 K:
         log fO2   -18     -15     -12      -9      -6
         X_H2      0.999   0.980   0.504   0.008   0.0002
         fex      -8.88   -8.71   -4.47   -0.067  -0.002
     Water dissociates to H2 + 1/2 O2 at low fO2 with no carbon anywhere. The identity holds
     because the SOLVER IS BYPASSED, not because the chemistry is trivial. Anything that runs the
     solver on a pure H-O system will see a large negative OFLUID, and any H2-solubility term
     (F.8) is live with carbon off and therefore needs its own switch, defaulting off.
   ! Protected by GATE 2a. The other place it is at risk is H_escape_mode - B.13.

 B.3  COMPOSITION BASE: EM_Xhz / EM_Xbs
 -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   defaults_include.f90 already holds the endmembers TOex uses (same 7 oxides, same order,
   canonically reordered in refstate.f90), so X = p*EM_Xbs + (1-p)*EM_Xhz ports verbatim.
   ? FEOPOS IS NOT USED. Under tracers_FeNum it holds Fe# = FeO/(FeO+MgO) against a DIFFERENT
     oxide basis (4 oxides, mole%, Xu et al. 2009). Mixing the two bases corrupts the Fe
     inventory, which is what converts O_ex <-> Fe3+. See F.1 for how to add it safely.

 B.4  TRACER FIELDS
 -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   RULE: every advected field must mean the same thing regardless of TYPEPOS. "Total carbon" and
   "excess oxygen" pass; "melt CO2" and "graphite" fail - they go silently wrong the moment a
   tracer melts or freezes.

   CONSERVED, ADVECTED, D = 1.0
       OEXPOS      wt% O   rock-hosted excess oxygen, SIGNED
       OxCPOS      wt% C   oxidised carbon  (carbonate on solid, dissolved CO2 on melt)
       ReCPOS      wt% C   reduced carbon   (graphite/diamond on solid, ~0 on melt)
       H2OFREEPOS  wt%     H in the free COH fluid, as H2O-EQUIVALENT
       CFLUIDPOS   wt% C   carbon in the free COH fluid
       OFLUIDPOS   wt% O   excess oxygen in the free COH fluid, SIGNED
   CONSERVED, REAL D        H2OPOS - bound water, keeps Dpartition_water
   WRITTEN EACH SOLVE       fO2POS, F3FTPOS  (diagnostic and warm start)
   UTILITY                  SCRBPOS, WSOLPOS - unchanged
   NEVER STORED, DERIVED    the five COH mole fractions, aC, graphite vs diamond, melt CO2 vs
                            solid carbonate (from TYPEPOS), fluid rho and eta

   ! H2OFREEPOS IS A HYDROGEN INVENTORY IN H2O-MASS UNITS. molH = 2*Mh/M_H2O is exact for H
     whatever the speciation (M_H2O already contains the oxygen, so Mh/M_H2O is H2O MOLECULES and
     the 2 is H atoms per molecule), and the solver divides by its own speciation-dependent nH.
     Measured at fixed Mh = 10 g, 3 GPa / 1500 K, sweeping fO2 from -14 to -1:
         H atoms      1.110186 mol   INVARIANT to 6 decimals
         fluid mass   4.34 -> 12.32 g   a 2.84x range that STRADDLES Mh
     It is not a one-sided bias - at the oxidised end the fluid is HEAVIER than Mh because it
     takes up CO2. H2OFREEPOS is not the fluid's mass in either direction; it is a hydrogen count
     at 9.0075 g per mol H.
     ! Nothing leaks. Water -> methane is a reaction, C + 2H2O -> CH4 + O2: the carbon comes from
       CFLUID and the liberated O2 is booked as excess oxygen, which is fex going negative. Mass
       closes across H2Ofree + CFLUID + OFLUID + OEX + OxC + ReC.
     ! No oxygen double-count, and not by luck: OFLUID is EXCESS O and H2O's oxygen is the
       reference, so it is excluded by construction.
     ! CONSUMERS THAT NEED THE REAL MASS, and there are only two: Darcy (3.E.3) and degassing
       (3.F.1). Both take n_fluid * mean molar mass from the solve; B.17 says why it is not stored.

   ? WHY OxC AND ReC ARE KEPT rather than collapsed to one total: melting.f90 feeds C(c_oxC) to
     the Dasgupta solidus (carbonate depresses it, graphite does not), and the pair preserves a
     kinetic degree of freedom for F.3. The redundancy with OEX is deliberate and safe under
     advection and under melting at D=1; it can break ONLY at mass-flux boundaries, which is
     exactly where B.6 is mandatory.

 B.4b ONE FIELD, SPLIT BY TRACER IDENTITY - NOT BY A SECOND FIELD
 -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   ? OEXPOS is ONE index shared across solid and melt tracers, not OEX_solid / OEX_melt. The
     separation "melt segregation carries its own Fe3+ and CO2 away" already exists: isol and imel
     are DIFFERENT tra entries from the moment of melting, each with its own scalar. A second
     field would sit at zero on every tracer, always.
   The split happens in two steps, both already in the plan:
     1  AT MELTING - mechanical, D = 1, mass-weighted. Bookkeeping only, no chemistry.
     2  AT THE NEXT CELL SOLVE - chemical. sOex and mOex are structurally distinct inside the
        residual, so the writeback re-equilibrates the mechanical split.
   After 2, imel carries its own re-equilibrated budget and takes it with it if it erupts - melt
   segregation of oxidised species, at no extra field cost.

 B.5  NOTHING IS DOUBLE-PARTITIONED
 -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   ! TOex IS the partitioning. Running C or O_ex through Dpartition would fractionate them and
     then let the solve fractionate them again from a corrupted start. No diagnostic.
   ? Water is genuinely different: TOex takes FREE water as an input and never splits H between
     melt and solid. Bound-water partitioning stays StagYY's job, so Dpartition_water stays.
   ? C and O_ex stay MECHANICALLY in the TE array with D forced to 1.0. At D=1 the fractional
     branch collapses to identity and the batch branch to mass-weighted mixing, so all six
     melting/freezing branches handle the transfer with no new code.
   ! The free COH fluid is a THIRD PHASE, not a dissolved component - a partition coefficient is
     the wrong tool for it entirely. D = 1 makes the melting step a mechanical split of pore
     fluid between the solid and melt sub-parcels. NO speciation factor belongs there; that is
     the double count this decision exists to prevent. The chemistry is in B.16 instead.
   ! Dpartition_carbon and outgas_fraction_carbon are REMOVED. Either silently reintroduces the
     double count, so setting them is a hard error.

 B.6  ONE REFERENCE-STATE HELPER
 -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
       toex_ref_state:  (mC, oex_per_C) -> (dC_ox, dC_red, dO_ex),  f_ox = clamp(oex_per_C/2, 0, 1)
   In volatilestuff, which is a leaf, so every call site can reach it: ingassing, eruptive
   outgassing, initial conditions, surface exchange, escape.

 B.7  SUB-GRID REDOX HETEROGENEITY MUST SURVIVE THE WRITEBACK
 -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   ? Grain size is NOT used anywhere in this work.
   ? WHAT PROTECTS THE HETEROGENEITY IS THE WRITEBACK RULE, not a timescale: 3.B.2 applies the
     cell's CHANGE to each tracer, so every tracer takes the same absolute increment and the spread
     between them is preserved exactly. Overwriting each tracer with the cell mean would flatten
     every cell every step, and 5.4 would then measure its own artefact.
   ! Melt sinks equilibrate essentially instantly; solid-state Fe3+ does not, by many orders of
     magnitude. That asymmetry is real but is deferred to F.9 - equilibration is instantaneous.

 B.8  ROBUSTNESS
 -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   ? Non-convergence: retain the previous step's partition for that cell, count it, report the
     rate. NEVER error stop - a production run is 1e4-1e6 steps.
   ? Isotonic repair of a non-monotonic oxybarometer happens OFFLINE at table-build time.
   ? Warm start the outer bisection within TOex_warmstart_window of the stored fO2POS, falling
     back to the full bracket on failure.
   ? Dirty-flag skipping: re-solve only when a cell has moved by more than TOex_d{T,phi,p,budget}_resolve.
   ? THE FLUID BRANCH IS NEVER SWITCHED OFF (corrected 2026-08-31). DHMS breakdown releases real
     free water beyond 10 GPa, and zeroing H at the solve while leaving it on the tracer is a mass
     violation. What actually breaks past 10 GPa is only the ZD09 FUGACITY EOS - K1..K4 and EMOG
     are HP98 and remain valid. So H2OFREE/CFLUID/OFLUID are tracked at every depth, and only the
     call into zd_volume/zd_fugacity clamps its (P,T) to the calibration box (<=10 GPa,
     673-2573 K), flagged by fluid_eos_extrapolated. Same technique as the solid table.

 B.9  THE SOLIDUS FEEDBACK IS LAGGED BY ONE STEP
 -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   Melting runs before the solve, so the carbonate fraction the solidus sees is from the previous
   one. ? Accepted and documented; the alternative is an inner iteration.

 B.10 SURFACE RESERVOIR: TWO BOXES, (C, O_ex) PAIRS
 -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   A  atmosphere + ocean, receives degassing.      State (C_A, O_A)
   B  ingassable crust/sediment, feeds ingassing.  State (C_B, O_B)
   ? The currency is (C, O_ex), not an ox/red binary: a binary cannot represent CH4, which is a
     reductant (O_ex = -2), not merely unoxidised.
   DIAGNOSTICS  Theta = O_A/C_A - 2 (surface oxidation excess: >0 oxic with free O2, =0 exactly
                CO2, -2<.<0 CO2+CO/CH4, <=-2 net reducing) and O_B/C_B (what gets subducted).

 B.11 BURIAL A -> B: TWO INDEPENDENT SCALARS
 -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   ! NEVER write burial as "move carbon at box A's prevailing O/C ratio". Written that way f_org
     does nothing and organic burial is meaningless.
       dC = burial carbon flux
       dO = 2*(1 - f_org)*dC*(M_O/M_C)          C_B += dC, O_B += dO, C_A -= dC, O_A -= dO
   The residual 2*f_org*dC*(M_O/M_C) STAYS IN A by construction. That one asymmetry is the entire
   redox engine of the surface system - it is how organic burial produces free O2.
   Two pathways f_org mixes: carbonate/seafloor carbonation (C enters B at O_ex = +2, neutral for
   A) and organic burial (C enters B at O_ex = 0; A keeps 2 O per C).
       RATE      F_bur = min( C_A*(1 - exp(-dt/tau_burial)), headroom(B) )
       CAPACITY  C_B <= (w_max_C/100)*M_ingassable
   ! The exponential matters: StagYY steps (1e3-1e5 yr) are comparable to plausible tau_burial, so
     a linear C_A*dt/tau would overshoot.
   ! When B saturates, burial backs up and C_A rises - a high-CO2 greenhouse. A result, not a
     clamp. Same constraint as the per-tracer ingassing cap (4.7); both must use the same w_max_C.

 B.12 THE DEGASSING / BURIAL STEADY STATE
 -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   With r_deg = O_ex/C of the erupted parcel, box A at steady state gives f_org = 1 - r_deg/2:
       r_deg = +2 (CO2)               f_org = 0     all burial must be carbonate
       r_deg = +1.6                   f_org = 0.2   ~ modern Earth
       r_deg =  0 (graphite-buffered) f_org = 1     all burial organic - the pump's maximum
       r_deg <  0 (CH4-rich)          f_org > 1     IMPOSSIBLE - no steady state
   ! The last row is a PREDICTION: a mantle reduced enough to degas net-reducing carbon cannot
     sustain an oxidising surface, and f_org <= 1 means you cannot tune out of it. For r_deg < 0
     the missing sink is hydrogen escape (B.13).
   ! STRUCTURAL WARNING - the loop through B and the mantle is POSITIVE (reduced mantle -> CH4-rich
     degassing -> reducing A -> more organic burial -> reduced B -> little oxidant ingassed ->
     mantle stays reduced), and symmetrically for the oxidised branch, so the coupled system is
     plausibly BISTABLE near r_deg = 0. Mitigating: mantle Fe3+ is a far larger oxygen reservoir
     than carbon, so loop gain may be small; and H escape is a one-way oxidising term that breaks
     the symmetry. Probed deliberately by 5.6.

 B.13 HYDROGEN ESCAPE
 -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   Because the reference puts H in H2O, escape bookkeeping is one line:
       EVERY 2 H ATOMS THAT ESCAPE LEAVE +1 O OF EXCESS OXYGEN BEHIND, REGARDLESS OF CARRIER.
       H2O degassed  dC 0, dO_ex 0    ... its 2 H escape  dC 0, dO_ex +1
       CH4 degassed  dC +1, dO_ex -2  ... its 4 H escape  dC 0, dO_ex +2   NET dC +1, dO_ex 0
   ! So methane degassing plus complete escape leaves carbon at GRAPHITE equivalence, not CO2.
     Getting the surface to CO2 still needs organic burial or further escape.
   ? PHYSICS: H2O is cold-trapped on a wet planet and barely escapes; CH4 and H2 are not and reach
     the homopause. That asymmetry is why a CH4-rich Archean atmosphere is thought to have driven
     net surface oxidation.
   ? H_escape_mode is REQUIRED, no silent default:
       'legacy'     water_budget(2) driven by outgas_fraction_water, exactly as today. Protects B.2.
       'speciated'  degassed H2O -> ocean; CH4/H2 -> escape; +1 O per 2 H credited to box A.
   ! Today outgas_fraction_water = 1.0 sends 100% of erupting tracers' water to space.
   ! PAYOFF: escape credits O to A, raises Theta, and (once f_org responds, F.2) lowers organic
     burial - a NEGATIVE feedback, and the term missing from B.12's loop. Not a refinement;
     plausibly what stabilises the system.

 B.14 SURFACE MODE
 -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   'twobox'      internal A/B model live, burial active.
   'prescribed'  C_B and O_B/C_B held at namelist values, burial = 0, A a pure diagnostic.
   ? 'prescribed' is the hook for an external climate model, and the control run for isolating
     mantle behaviour from surface feedback - which is what 5.6 needs.
   ! Degassing speciation is computed in BOTH modes: it is a by-product of the cell solve on the
     mantle side. f_org, tau_burial and w_max_C are 'twobox' parameters only.

 B.15 f_org IS CONSTANT FOR NOW
 -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   ? A single namelist scalar.
   ! Consequence: unless f_org happens to equal 1 - r_deg/2, box A drifts monotonically. Theta
     must be in the time series. Feedback formulations that would bound it are in F.2.

 B.16 WATER ACTIVITY COUPLES SOLUBILITY TO SPECIATION   (settled 2026-09-04)
 -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   THE PROBLEM. WaterSaturationAndVerticalRedistribution dissolves free fluid into rock as bound
   water up to the storage capacity S. But the sH2O tables were generated with
   Initialize_MAGEMin(buffer="aH2O") at B = 1, i.e. saturation against PURE WATER. Once the fluid
   is COH with X_H2O < 1, water activity is lower and the true storage content is lower. The same
   applies in reverse: with a reduced fluid present, MORE water should exsolve, not less.

   ? THE FIX IS ON THE THRESHOLD, NOT ON THE FLUX.
         S_eff = S * a_H2O^n,     a_H2O = (phi_H2O/phi_H2O_pure)*X_H2O  ~  X_H2O
     ! Capping the transferable MASS at X_H2O instead was considered and REJECTED: it makes the
       amount moved per step depend on speciation while leaving the equilibrium target at S, so an
       undersaturated tracer still walks to the wrong endpoint, just more slowly.
   ? n is a namelist knob defaulting to 1, because linear-in-activity is what the table's own
     buffer axis means. NAM literature supports 0.5-1 depending on the substitution mechanism.
   ? LAGGED ONE STEP, from the speciation cached at 3.C.6. a_H2O comes FROM the solve, so using it
     to set S_eff in the same step is circular. a_H2O = 1 before the first solve, so behaviour is
     unchanged until then. Standard operator splitting; a_H2O tracks C/H and fO2, both slow.
   ? BOTH SOLID AND MELT take the scaling, but they are NOT equally clean:
       SOLID   NAMs take H as OH point defects; several substitution mechanisms, so the exponent
               is uncertain, but there is no meaningful H2/CH4 storage. One activity variable
               genuinely controls it - a_H2O^n is the right functional form.
       MELT    dissolves H2O as molecular + OH, but ALSO molecular H2 and CH4, and those matter
               precisely where a_H2O is low (Hirschmann et al. 2012; Ardia et al. 2013).
     ! KNOWN LIMITATION, ACCEPTED: the scaling drives melt water -> 0 as X_H2O -> 0, where H2/CH4
       dissolution should take over. Reduced-fluid melt is therefore UNDER-hydrated. Documented
       rather than floored - an invented floor is a number nobody can defend later. Closing it
       needs an H2 solubility model, which is F.8.
   ! Carbon-off is inert by B.2: X_H2O == 1, so S_eff == S identically and GATE 2a/3a are safe.
   ! The rigorous alternative is an a_H2O axis on StagH2O.dat, exactly as fO2 got its R axis.
     Deferred: expensive, and the cheap version should first show that it matters.

 B.17 ONE ELEMENT CURRENCY; REAL MASS IS DERIVED, NEVER STORED   (settled 2026-09-04)
 -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

       water_budget   H, in H2O-EQUIVALENT kg      carbon_budget  C      oxygen_budget  O_ex
   Two of the three already worked this way; only the H ledger's UNITS need explaining, and those
   units are load-bearing - they are what makes carbon-off numerically unchanged.

   ? REJECTED, with reasons, so they are not revisited:
     true element masses (wt% H)   cleanest semantics, but every threshold
       (water_percolation_threshold, WaterSatCutoff, water_min_sat, water_dry_conc, topH2O_val),

       for a cosmetic gain, and GATE 2a fails by construction.
     species-resolved tracer fields  underdetermined per tracer (F.7), AND not independently
       conserved - the species interconvert, so every solve would overwrite them.
     an advected fluid-MASS field    fluid mass is not conserved under a re-solve (same H,

 B.18 THE FLUID MOVES AS AN ELEMENT TRIPLE, NEVER AS A REDOX LABEL   (settled 2026-09-04)
 -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   THE PROBLEM. Free fluid is the one volatile reservoir that moves by something other than
   advection: WaterSaturationAndVerticalRedistribution marches a scalar in kg up each column,
   handing it between cells and between subdomains. A parcel leaving a reduced cell and arriving
   in an oxidised one must deliver the right C and O, not just H.

   ? "OXIDISED / NEUTRAL / REDUCED" IS NOT TRANSPORTABLE. The same fluid is oxidising in one cell
     and reducing in another - the label is a RELATION between fluid and host, not a property of
     the fluid. What is transportable is the element budget, and B.4 already provides it:
         (H2Ofree, CFLUID, OFLUID) = (H as H2O-equivalent, C, excess O signed)
     Three extensive amounts, complete, and MIXING IS EXACT ELEMENT-WISE ADDITION - no mixing rule
     for fO2, no lever arithmetic, no averaging of an intensive variable. That is the reason this
     basis was chosen, and it is what makes the receiving cell's job trivial: it does not need to
     know where the parcel came from, only the three numbers.

   THE VALIDITY WINDOW, in mol:        -n_H/2  <=  n_Oex  <=  +2*n_C
     Upper bound: all C as CO2, and H cannot oxidise past H2O. Lower bound: has NO carbon term,
     because CH4 (-2 per C over 4 H) and H2 (-1 per H2) are both exactly -0.5 per H - the most
     reducing thing available to a fluid is a function of its hydrogen alone. A parcel outside the
     window cannot exist. Position INSIDE it is the redox state, and it is a pure function of the
     three transported numbers, computable with no thermodynamics.

   ? TWO TRANSPORT RULES, AND THEY DIFFER:
     DISSOLUTION  pool -> bound water.  H ONLY. It takes zero C and - because H2O is the reference
       state - ZERO O_ex, so the ledger closes with no extra term. The pool's C/H rises as a
       consequence, which is correct: stripping water out of a COH fluid drives the residue toward
       carbon saturation.
     PHASE SPLIT  pool -> {stays, ascends}.  CONGRUENT, fixed proportions on all three. Darcy moves
       a PHASE; there is no basis for fractionating elements at a permeability limit.
   ! The failure mode of the first rule - a parcel stripped of H arriving as bare carbon - is shut
     off by B.16, not by a clamp: as the pool dries of H2O, a_H2O falls, S_eff falls, dissolution
     stops. B.16 IS THEREFORE LOAD-BEARING FOR CONSERVATION, not only for realism, and is a
     prerequisite of 3.E rather than an independent refinement.
   ! This also answers "can only part of the free H2O be partitioned per step" - the question is
     malformed. Every kilogram that moves is FLUID, and fluid is the triple. The speciation
     limitation lives on the capacity (B.16), never on the flux.

   ! THE BOUND-WATER DIFFUSION PATH NEEDS NOTHING (advdif -> smooth_tracer_H2O). It moves c_h2o
     only, which is pure H2O, hence zero O_ex by construction.

   ! ncycles = 3 LETS A PARCEL CROSS THE MANTLE IN ONE STEP with its composition frozen at the
     deepest source's speciation. It still exchanges mass with every cell on the way, but no
     intermediate cell re-speciates it, so redox freezing - carbon precipitating where a rising
     reduced fluid meets oxidised wall rock - cannot form at all. The ncycles = 4 path
     (limitflow_relperm) caps transit at ONE cell per step, which makes "inert in transit" exactly
     consistent, because the receiving cell's next solve IS the equilibration.
     ? limitflow_relperm is REQUIRED with carbon on (3.E.6). Same one-step-lag class as B.9, but
       only if transit is one cell.

 F.1  FEOPOS Fe-INVENTORY CORRECTION. Phase 1 uses endmember Fe only. Once Fe# drifts through
      melting the ceilings maxsOex/maxmOex are affected. Add as a RELATIVE correction, never
      absolute:  n_Fe = n_Fe(EM at p) * [Fe#_tracer / Fe#_endmember(p)], with the denominator on
      the SAME 4-oxide Xu basis the tracer's Fe# lives on. Being a ratio of ratios, the basis
      mismatch cancels - the only safe formulation across two incompatible oxide sets.

 F.2  f_org FEEDBACK. Three candidates: (i) constant, current; (ii) relax toward 1 - r_deg/2,
      bounded but circular - it imposes the steady state one would rather predict; (iii)
      f_org = f_org_max*sigma(-Theta/k), since organic preservation is strongly enhanced under
      anoxic bottom water. RECOMMENDED when the time comes; reduces to (i) as k -> infinity.
      ! (iii) regulates A's oxygen but does NOT damp the loop through B and the mantle. H escape does.

 F.3  KINETIC OxC <-> ReC. Graphite -> carbonate is not instantaneous. The pair is currently
      slaved to the equilibrium answer; carrying it as genuine state with a relaxation is a
      one-line change once a timescale is defensible. This is why the fields were kept (B.4).

 F.4  B -> A WEATHERING RETURN. Interface at 4.10, rate defaulted to zero. Carbonate weathering
      returns C at O_ex = +2 (redox-neutral); oxidative weathering of organic C returns it at
      O_ex = 0 while CONSUMING 2 O per C from A - the exact inverse of the organic pump, and A's
      main oxygen sink. ! Without it, and with f_org constant, A's only oxygen sink is reduced
      degassing.

 F.5  MELT COMPOSITION CONSISTENCY. TOex mixes the SOLID endmembers by p while holding the melt at
      a fixed basaltic XB. In StagYY the melt composition also varies with ttype. A conscious
      approximation, not an oversight.

 F.6  phi_i(P,T) EOS SEED TABLE. Only worth building if profiling shows the EOS loop dominating.

 F.7  PER-TRACER COH SPECIATION. Not needed and not built. (H, C, O_ex) is a COMPLETE element
      budget, but three amounts against five species with four degrees of freedom is
      underdetermined by two; the missing constraints are thermodynamic, so recovering speciation
      needs a 2-D solve for (fO2, aC). Never required, because the cell-level call already
      produces speciation everywhere it is used, and B.18 shows transport does not need it either.
      If it is ever wanted, that 2-D solve is the route - not more tracer fields.

 F.8  H2 AND CH4 SOLUBILITY IN MELT. B.16's a_H2O scaling drives melt water to zero as X_H2O -> 0,
      where molecular H2/CH4 dissolution should take over (Hirschmann et al. 2012; Ardia et al.
      2013). Until a solubility law is added, reduced-fluid melt is UNDER-hydrated. Accepted and
      documented rather than floored.

 F.9  TIME-DEPENDENT SOLID EQUILIBRATION. Melt sinks equilibrate essentially instantly; solid-
      state Fe3+ does not, by many orders of magnitude. The form is a rate-limited exchange toward
      the cell solution, dOEX_applied = dOEX*(1 - exp(-dt/tau_redox_solid)), on the SOLID increment
      only. Two things to get right when it is built:
      ! IT IS A TRANSFER, NOT A PARTIAL APPLICATION. If the solid takes less oxygen than the solve
        says, the remainder must be somewhere - credit it to OFLUID explicitly. Applying a fraction
        of the solid's change while leaving the other reservoirs at their full new values loses
        exactly the un-relaxed amount, every cell, every step.
      ! A relaxing cell is deliberately OUT of equilibrium and therefore dirty by construction.
        Exclude it from 3.C.5's skip test, or tau silently becomes infinite.
